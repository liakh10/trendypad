Deploy Trendypad

Everything below is sent from your own wallet on Robinhood Chain. That wallet becomes the owner of the factory and the buyback and the buyback keeper until you change them. Progress is remembered in this browser

  1. TrendyToken implementation, cloned for every token
  2. TrendyPad implementation, cloned for every pad
  3. TrendyFactory with the Gentle, Standard and Steep curves and a 0.5% protocol fee
  4. TrendyRouter for ETH trades
  5. TrendyBuyback for $TRENDY
  6. Connect the router and the buyback to the factory, each can be set once
  7. Allow the Uniswap Universal Router as the buyback swap target
  8. Put the three addresses into config.js and push
